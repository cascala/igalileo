For teachers

Teacher toolkit

Everything works from a link. Students need a browser and nothing else; their work is saved in their own browser.

Lesson packets

One packet per course: the lesson notebooks plus practice sets. Share one link and the whole class opens it in JupyterLite.

Practice with a version per student

Each student types their name once and gets their own numbers. They check themselves as they go.

seed("Ana Lopez")
p = randprime(50, 100); q = randprime(50, 100)

Answer key

Paste your class list and get each student's numbers and answers on one page.

Make your own lesson

A template in the same style as our lessons (idea, worked example, try it, exercises) and a short guide to filling it in.

templateguide

Grading when AI is everywhere

A short, frank page: take-home work is practice. Grade in class, out loud, or by asking students to explain each step. Galileo's step-by-step notes help with that.
