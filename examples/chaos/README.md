# Learning path: chaos and fractals

Two lessons on simple rules with unpredictable results, and the shapes they make.

| # | Notebook | What you learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Chaos](01-chaos.ipynb) | iterating a rule, fixed points and their stability, the logistic map, period doubling, the bifurcation diagram, Feigenbaum's constant, sensitive dependence, the Lorenz butterfly |
| 2 | [Fractals](02-fractals.ipynb) | the Koch snowflake, fractal dimension, the Mandelbrot set and zooming into it, Julia sets |

Prerequisites: the [foundations](../foundations/README.md), especially complex numbers and differential equations.

The notebooks are generated from the plain-text sources in [`../src/chaos`](../src/chaos) by
`python3 examples/src/build.py`, then executed so that results and plots are stored with them.
