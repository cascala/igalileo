# Reading research papers

Lessons that take a recent research paper and build its ideas from the ground up, with computations you can check
along the way. Each one prepares you to read the paper itself; none replaces it.

| # | Notebook | Paper | What you learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Quantum gravity from the null cone](01-quantum-gravity-null-cone.ipynb) | V. Chandrasekaran, "An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone", [arXiv:2611.34924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34924) (2026) | black hole entropy, causal diamonds, the 2D toy model and its clock, focusing and an area bound, orthonormal clock states, where $A/4G\hbar$ enters |

Prerequisites: the [general relativity](../general-relativity/README.md) path through black holes, and basic quantum
mechanics.

The notebooks are generated from the plain-text sources in [`../src/papers`](../src/papers) by
`python3 examples/src/build.py`, then executed so that results and plots are stored with them.
